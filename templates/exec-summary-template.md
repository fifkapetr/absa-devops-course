# Executive summary: INC-<YYYYMMDD>-<nn>

Half a page for management. No log lines, no file names, no individuals, no applicant data. Times in CEST.

**What happened.** Two or three sentences: the system, the branch, the date, what users saw and for how long.

**Customer impact.** Minutes of degraded or lost service during business hours; number of applications and decisions affected; what the branch did meanwhile (paper process). Numbers cite the postmortem.

**Cause in one sentence.** The underlying reason, in plain language.

**What we changed.** The immediate fix (rollback, configuration) and the permanent fix with its status (merged, in review, planned).

**Risk and compliance.** Data lost or exposed, Compliance informed when, regulatory or policy references (POL-070), remaining exposure.

**Decisions needed.** Bullet list of decisions that need management: budget, policy change, go or no-go for the next pilot step, with the recommended option.
