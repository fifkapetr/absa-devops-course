# Executive summary: INC-20261005-01

**What happened.** On Monday 2026-10-05, the CZ-PRG-01 branch pilot of Tredgate Loan was updated to version 1.2.0 at 08:30 CEST. From 09:12 CEST the loan-API service crashed and restarted nine times over 67 minutes; each restart caused approximately 40–60 seconds of service errors. Officers saw "Request failed with status 502" errors on the installment preview, the application list, and on at least one loan decision. A rollback to version 1.1.0 was completed at 10:20 CEST and the service has run without errors since then. The incident was declared resolved at 11:30 CEST.

**Customer impact.** The branch experienced intermittent service failures during the 67-minute window from 09:12 CEST to 10:20 CEST. 1,808 of 22,717 API requests returned 502 errors during this period (7.96%). Failures were brief (~40–60 s) and separated by normal operation (~8 min), allowing most work to continue. No applications were lost: the application count was confirmed at 12 after the rollback, consistent with the pre-incident count plus applications entered during the morning. No paper re-entry was required.

**Cause in one sentence.** Release 1.2.0 introduced a calculation cache that stored a new entry on every keystroke (because the cache key included the current time), filled the container's 256 MB memory limit in approximately 42 minutes, and was killed and restarted automatically — repeating the cycle nine times.

**What we changed.** The service was rolled back to version 1.1.0 at 10:20 CEST, restoring full service immediately. The installment-preview feature introduced in 1.2.0 is unavailable until the permanent fix is reviewed and deployed. The Developer has described a fix: correct the cache key, add an eviction policy, and remove the non-compliant log line. The fix requires a data-and-logging review (OPS-050) with Compliance Officer sign-off before it can be merged. A postmortem and Known Issue entry (KI-005) are due by 2026-10-12 per OPS-040.

**Risk and compliance.** Release 1.2.0 wrote the full request body — including applicant names — to the container log in every calculation request (12,169 lines, from 08:30 to 10:20 CEST). This violates OPS-060 (no personal data in logs) and triggered the POL-070 same-day notification requirement. The Compliance Officer was informed at 10:10 CEST on the day of the incident. The pilot-host log containing applicant names is being treated as personal data; it has not been distributed beyond the investigation team. The count of distinct applicants whose names were logged has not yet been determined and must be provided to the Compliance Officer.

**Decisions needed.**

- **Approve the 1.2.x fix pull request** — the Developer's proposed fix (corrected cache key, LRU eviction, compliant log message) requires Compliance Officer sign-off under OPS-050 data-and-logging review before it can be merged and redeployed.
- **Obtain distinct-applicant count** — the number of unique applicant names in the 1.2.0 log must be extracted (with redaction) and reported to the Compliance Officer for the personal-data register; a decision on any further notification or remediation steps may follow.
- **Clarify the restart mechanism** — sources disagree on whether automatic container restarts are configured in `compose.yaml` or provided by a host watchdog cron; the authoritative mechanism should be confirmed, documented, and made consistent so that its behaviour in a future incident is predictable.
